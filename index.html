Prototype assumptions

Legal transfer: 5 minutes or more between trains. Practical routing: can also surface 4-minute and 1–3-minute connections, clearly marked as non-protected.

For the first deadhead home, the clock starts at the scheduled arrival of Job 166's final working train, not the printed release time. This lets the prototype correctly recognize the weekday 5-minute connection to 2702 and the weekend 2-minute connection to 8702.
