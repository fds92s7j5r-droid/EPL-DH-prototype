What this layer is testing

Legal route home: the first public deadhead must depart at or after the printed release, and every train-to-train transfer must be at least 5 minutes. To-work arrival: this sandbox currently allows arrival exactly at report time, but flags the margin in the result so we can decide whether EPL should require an additional report buffer.

Practical route home: the sandbox may also inspect service after the final working-train arrival. Anything before printed release or with a transfer under 5 minutes is clearly marked non-protected. A verified Community Deadhead Note can explain why EPL still surfaces it.

Job 166 seed note: weekend 8702 is intentionally marked as a verified real-world connection so we can test the exact scenario that inspired this layer.
